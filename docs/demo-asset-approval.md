# Altovena – real product screenshots: approval checklist

Use actual captured screens **only after human review**. These are not automatically pulled from application repos.

## Required source material

| Product | Best first screenshot | Context to include | Review owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| Offer Intelligence | Overview of a company dossier, with separate data quality and interest indicators | Explain public data sources, uncertainty, human approval and non-automated outreach | Freddy |
| Visibility | Prioritized technical/content audit view | Show source basis, proposed action, review status and measured result where available | Erlend Thorsnes Karlsen |

## Screenshot acceptance criteria

1. Captured from a currently functioning environment, with date and version recorded internally.
2. Mask all customer names, emails, phone numbers, business-sensitive pricing, tokens, property IDs and financial figures unless authorized for public disclosure.
3. Make **real UI versus mock illustration** explicit. Do not composite invented KPI figures into a real screenshot.
4. Verify image rights, alt text, compressed size, mobile cropping and accessibility.
5. Only publish a claim that matches verified runtime behavior. No public availability claim for an internal pilot.
6. Obtain review from the relevant product owner, Andrea for presentation, Erlend for SEO/AEO/GEO content and Anna for financial/compliance disclosures.
7. Prefer internal images stored in this repository after approval rather than remote protected application URLs.

## Contact / lead integration prerequisite

The current demo brief opens a local email draft. Do not substitute a fake CRM success state. A real integration will need a documented Nexus Sales intake endpoint, authorization and anti-spam strategy, consent text/privacy policy, environment-scoped secrets, access-controlled storage and failure observability. Build and test in preview before production.
