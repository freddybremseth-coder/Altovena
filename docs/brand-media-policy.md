# Altovena Brand Media Policy · v0.6

This plan is the required standard for adding images, video, and screenshots to Altovena.

1. **Real system screenshots** come only from actual functioning environments, with image-owner approval and client data redacted. No fabricated numbers, customer messages, email addresses, tokens, parcel identifiers or performance claims.
2. **AI-generated product images** are visually useful, but must be labelled as *concept illustrations* — never "screen captures", "live dashboards" or customer results. Descriptive, subject-focused alt text is required.
3. **Brand asset placement**: Altovena wordmark in header/footer; simplified ribbon monogram as favicon; light Mediterranean technology hero on home; one product-specific illustration each on Offer Intelligence, Visibility, RealtyFlow, Olivia; Benidorm concept only on contact/company and explicitly not a photo of Eagle Tower premises.
4. **Video**: an 18-second, captioned, silent visual introduction for the home page. All panels represent brand/product directions, not recordings of a working product. Video defaults to user-started controls, preload none, and a compressed poster. Do not autoplay with audio.
5. **Performance/accessibility**: optimized WebP and appropriate image width/height, lazy loading except hero, no text baked into website illustrations, keyboard-accessible video controls. Contrast must be checked in browser.
6. **Website facts**: Altovena.com is the proposed public domain supplied by the owner. Contact address supplied by owner: Avenida del Presidente Adolfo Suárez 14, Eagle Tower, Benidorm, Alicante, Spain. Do not label it the legally registered office without documentation. Legal entity, tax number and privacy statements still need verification.
7. **Release gate**: human approval of brand images and real demos, and review by Andrea (communication/UX), Erlend (SEO/AEO/GEO), Anna (financial/legal disclosures) and Freddy (technical/product truth). Preserve preview noindex while incomplete.

A real product screenshot may **replace** a concept illustration later only after the relevant acceptance checks pass; both types must remain distinguishable.
